About Intensity Tracking

Intensity measures how much of your running is done at a high effort level.

Intensity is calculated using heart rate and pace:

  • If your heart rate is above your threshold, that time counts as intensity
  • If your pace is faster than your VDOT-based easy pace threshold, that time counts as intensity
  • If either of these conditions is met, that section of the run is counted as intensity

This approach ensures that intensity is captured whether you're running hard by heart rate (e.g., hot conditions) or by pace (e.g., flat tempo runs). The system uses the Daniels VDOT system to determine your easy pace threshold from your race performance.
